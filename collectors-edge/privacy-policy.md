# The Collector's Edge Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** October 6, 2026

The Collector's Edge is a private, locally hosted inventory and listing-management application used by its owner to manage a personal sports-card and collectibles collection and the owner's own eBay seller account.

## Information the application processes

The application may process inventory information entered or imported by the user, including card or collectible descriptions, certification numbers, grades, images, pricing information, notes, and listing details.

When the owner connects an eBay account, the application may process OAuth credentials and information needed to manage the owner's own eBay listings, inventory, business policies, and related seller functions.

Optional image-search and verification features may send card metadata, search queries, or candidate images to third-party services selected and configured by the user, including eBay, PSA, OpenAI, Google Gemini, Brave Search, or other public web sources.

## Storage

The application is designed to run locally on the owner's computer. Inventory data, application settings, and authentication information are stored locally on that device unless the owner explicitly exports or transfers them.

The application is not operated as a hosted multi-user service and does not maintain accounts or profiles for other eBay users.

## eBay data

The application accesses eBay only with the authorization of the owner of the connected eBay account. It is intended to manage that owner's own seller inventory and listings.

The application does not intentionally collect or maintain personal information about unrelated eBay buyers, sellers, or other third parties. It does not operate a separate database of third-party eBay user profiles.

## Sharing and sale of information

The application does not sell personal information.

Information is shared with external services only when necessary to perform a feature the owner has chosen to use, such as authenticating with eBay, retrieving card information, searching for images, verifying an image, or creating an eBay listing. Those external services process information under their own privacy policies and terms.

## Security

Credentials and API keys are intended to remain on the owner's local device. The owner is responsible for safeguarding the computer, API credentials, OAuth tokens, and any exported files.

## Data retention and deletion

Because the application is locally hosted, the owner controls retention of the local database, images, exports, credentials, and configuration files. Local application data can be removed by deleting the application's data files and revoking connected-service credentials or OAuth access with the applicable provider.

## Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if application functionality or connected services change. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are made.
